# StarFolio · Public shelf

[中文](README.md) · [English](README.en.md)

A selected public collection, separate from the [StarFolio application](https://github.com/StarDreamTech/StarFolio) and from the private library on any device. Contains 212 published blog articles and 108 historical X posts with 1501 visible replies recorded in the source. Archive counts are not a claim of complete account or comment history.

Download and unzip this repository. Open **index.html** for offline reading, or run `folio import-collection <directory>` in StarFolio 0.2+. Keep the `read/`, `media/` and stylesheet files together. The catalog contains original URLs, record checksums, media mappings and missing sources; individual X records retain the original v3 JSON. No login, auto-upload, hosted analytics or membership content. Original authors retain their rights.
